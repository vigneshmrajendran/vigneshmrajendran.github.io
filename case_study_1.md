## Business Case Study - 1: (F500 Client Optimization):
  - Problem: Post-M&A cash crisis, needed to cut costs without killing revenue
  - Approach: Constrained optimization to identify low-margin/high-cost clients
  - Result: 45% cost reduction for the managed customers segment while maintaining 92% revenue
  - Description: optimised the existing clientele of a fortune 500 company's managed partner user base (think sme clients with significant business) to minimize for profit margin impact while maintaining the impact on revenue using numerical optimization techniques (non linear programming with constraints and optimization function). The f500 company was under heavy cash crunch due to an m&a effort at global scale with it's key competition due to Amazon's rising prominence.
  - Tools: Python (scipy.optimize), pandas, visualization
