## Business Case Study - 2: (Social Media ROI Engine):
- Problem: Startup needed engagement with limited ad budget
- Approach: Conditional probability word engine for content optimization
- Result: virality achieved in 35% of social media posts and increased the overall customer engagement by close to 212%
- Description: created a conditional probability based word recommendation engine to optimize for increasing customer engagement with social media content for a low cost startup wanting to increase social media outreach under constrained budget and time-line. No bayesian inference, no heavy compute, no llm (may be irrelevant in today's llm powered era but back then this was such a elegantly simpleand powerful solution to a complex and licrative problem)
- Tools: Python, traditional NLP techniques, statistical modeling
