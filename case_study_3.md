## Business Case Study - 3: (Donor Profiling & Segmentation for Targeted Ad campaigns)
- Problem: Global Non-Profit needed to optimize marketing spend by understanding its donor base
- Approach: Donor Profiling, Segmentation and Targeted ad spend to maximize donor contributions per dolloar of marketing spend
- Result: marketing spend yielded 67% more donations based on these efforts
- Description: have worked on a probono project for a global non-profit to profile donors (based on collected data about payment patterns), segment their donors (using aggomerative clustering and validating that output through various techniques like sum of squares, elbow curve etc.), and improve their marketing campaigns through the insights from profiling and segmentation of their donors to maximize contributions based on seasonality, profiling and optimal marketing triggers.
- Tools: R, Clustering, tidyverse, data.table, Linear Optimization based ad targeting
